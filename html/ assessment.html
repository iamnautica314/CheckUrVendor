🏢 Vendor Information

Enter ur vendor's basic information

🔐 Identity & Access Management

Evaluate how the vendor manages user identities, authentication, authorization, and access to sensitive systems

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Does the vendor enforce MFA (Multi Factor Authentication) for users with access to sensitive systems and data?

Least Privilege

Does the vendor enforce least privilege access and regularly monitor user activity?

Access Reviews

Are user access reviews performed regularly to ensure permissions remain appropriate?

🔒 Data Protection

Evaluate how the vendor protects sensitive information during storage, transmission, and recovery

Encryption at Rest

Is sensitive data encrypted while stored (at rest)?

Encryption in Transit

Is sensitive data encrypted while transmitted across networks?

Backup & Recovery

Are backups performed regularly and protected against unauthorized modification?

📋 Governance & Compliance

Evaluate the vendor's governance practices, compliance posture, and organizational cybersecurity policies.

Information Security Policies

Does the vendor maintain documented information security policies and procedures?

Policies should define how information is protected, handled, and accessed throughout the organization.

SOC 2 / Independent Security Assessment

Does the vendor maintain a current SOC 2 report or equivalent independent security assessment?

Security Awareness Training

Are employees required to complete regular cybersecurity awareness training?

⚙️ Change & Vulnerability Management

Evaluate how the vendor manages system changes, vulnerabilities, and security updates.

Change Management

Does the vendor maintain a formal change management process for systems, applications, and networks?

Vulnerability Management

Does the vendor regularly identify, assess, and remediate security vulnerabilities?

Patch Management

Does the vendor maintain current security patches and secure system configurations?

🌐 Network Security

Evaluate the effectiveness of the vendor's network security controls.

Network Security Controls

Are network security controls implemented to prevent unauthorized access?

Threat Detection

Are intrusion detection and prevention controls implemented to identify malicious activity?

Penetration Testing

Are independent penetration tests performed on a regular basis?

🚨 Incident Response

Evaluate the vendor's ability to detect, respond to, and recover from cybersecurity incidents.

Incident Response Plan

Does the vendor maintain a documented cybersecurity incident response plan?

Incident Response Testing

Is the incident response plan tested regularly through tabletop exercises or simulations?

Security Incidents

Has the vendor experienced any significant cybersecurity incidents or data breaches within the past 12 months?

🔄 Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

Evaluate the vendor's ability to continue operations and recover following disruptive events.

Disaster Recovery Plan

Does the vendor maintain a documented Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)?

Business Continuity Plan

Does the vendor maintain and regularly test a Business Continuity Plan (BCP)?
